======================================================================
  EnhancedDynamics v1.0.0
======================================================================

Enhances KSP's external flight camera with dynamic physics inertia,
G-force tracking, and customizable visual feedback.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
  CONTROLS & CONFIGURATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
* Press [F8] or [Numpad +] while in-flight to open the settings menu.
* Keybinds can also be remapped inside the mod's config file.
* All settings save automatically per installation.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
  INSTALLATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Place the EnhancedDynamics folder directly into your KSP gamedata directory:

  Kerbal Space Program/GameData/EnhancedDynamics/

----------------------------------------------------------------------
  MOTION SICKNESS & ACCESSIBILITY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
This mod includes dynamic camera movement. If you experience any 
cybersickness or visual discomfort, uninstall the mod or use it 
in-game occasionally rather than permanently.
